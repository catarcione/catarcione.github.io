Conrado Catarcione

PhD student · PESC | COPPE | UFRJ

My research interests lie in Network Science and its applications to epidemiology and infectious disease control. In particular, my work focuses on the role of asymptomatic individuals during epidemic outbreaks, investigating how their presence influences disease transmission dynamics and the effectiveness of public health interventions and control strategies.
